How we choose gamesOur editorial approach

We make web games at onholdlab. These are our selection and writing principles.

Science you play with
Physics, maths, code or biology must shape the mechanics. A sci-fi theme alone is not enough.
Show the mechanic
Concrete descriptions and our own diagrams, without borrowing game screenshots.
Know before you play
Platform, price, languages and availability. Anything unconfirmed is marked as such.
Fewer, deeper
3–5 games a week, with the searches that came up empty recorded too.
